Assemblage

Describe what you're modelling. Blender tells you what it's called and builds you a reference board.

You know what it looks like, not what it's called. Assemblage finds the name, then the pictures.

Install in one drag

Free, for Blender 5.2 or newer

  1. 1Open Blender 5.2 or newer.
  2. 2Drag your button below onto the Blender window.
  3. 3Click Install. If Blender asks to allow online access, allow it: Assemblage needs it to search.
  4. 4In the 3D viewport, press N and open the Assemblage tab.

On a phone? Blender runs on your computer, so send yourself this page and install it there.

Download AssemblageChoose the zip for your system

Prefer a file? Click a button instead: the zip downloads, then drag the zip into Blender. On a Mac with Safari, if the download unzips itself, right-click the button and choose Download Linked File.

Suggest names

Describe it in plain words and get the names used on product pages, with a hint for each.

Picks the best

Searches the web, drops tiny and blurry images, and ticks the most useful ones.

Boards in one click

Lay the picks out next to your model in Blender, or send them to PureRef.

Does it generate images?

No. It finds real photos and ranks them with an AI model that runs on your computer. The model is an optional one-time 608 MB download; searching works without it.

What does it cost?

Nothing. It's free and open source under the GPL.

What leaves my computer?

Your search goes to DuckDuckGo and Wikimedia Commons. If you use Suggest Names, your description is sent to Assemblage's server to come up with names, and it isn't stored.

How do I update it?

Come back here and drag the button in again. Blender replaces the old version.
