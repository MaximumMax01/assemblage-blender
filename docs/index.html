Using it

Three steps, all inside Blender.

1Type what you're modelling

Be specific. The more you say, the better the references.

  • ✗chair
  • ✓black mesh office chair

Want it shown somewhere real? Say where.

  • ✓drinking fountain on a hallway wall

That brings back installed fountains instead of catalog shots on white.

Don't know what it's called?

Describe it, like rubber strip where office walls meet the floor, and click Suggest Names. Click the one you mean.

  • Rubber cove base
  • Vinyl wall base
  • Skirting board

2Search, then check

Images that are too small, blurry or duplicated are dropped, the rest are ranked, and the best few come ticked.

Untick what you don't want. Tick anything it missed.

3Send the board

Send to Blender lays the picks out in a side viewport, well away from your model. Send to PureRef saves a .pur file and opens it.

Good to know

It finds photos, it doesn't make them

Every result is a real image from the web. The model that ranks them runs on your computer, and is an optional one-time 608 MB download checked against a fingerprint. Searching works without it.

How it ranks

Each image is scored with CLIP on two questions: is it the thing you asked for, and is it a useful reference rather than a wide scene, collage or watermarked stock photo? The wording and weights were tuned against hand-labelled searches. See scoring.py in the source.

What leaves your computer

Your search goes to DuckDuckGo and Wikimedia Commons, and images come from the sites that host them. Suggest Names sends your description to Assemblage's server, which runs an open model and doesn't store it. Nothing else is sent.

Whose images these are

Images belong to their owners. Use them as references, not in published work.

Requirements

Blender 5.2 or newer, on 64-bit Windows or macOS 14+ on Apple Silicon. Allow online access when Blender asks, or turn it on in Preferences > System > Network.

Downloading instead of dragging

Clicking a button downloads the zip; drag the zip into Blender. In Safari, if the zip unzips itself, right-click the button and choose Download Linked File.

Updating

Come back and drag the button in again. Blender replaces the old version.
